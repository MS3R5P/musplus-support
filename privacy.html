MUSPLUS Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 27, 2026

The short version: your music library stays on your device, and we don't collect anything.

MUSPLUS (the "App") is developed by Mark Pticar ("we", "us"). This policy describes what happens to your data when you use the App.

What the App stores, and where

  • Your music — the audio files you import, their metadata (title, artist, album, genre) and artwork — is copied into the App's own storage on your device. It is never uploaded to us or to any third-party service.
  • Playlists and playback settings — your Up Next queue, playlists, equalizer presets, crossfade setting and theme — are stored locally on your device.
  • Sync Music transfers song files directly between your own devices over your local Wi-Fi network, device-to-device, using Apple's Multipeer Connectivity framework. It never passes through any server operated by us, and nothing leaves your local network.

What the App asks permission for

  • Local network access — only used for Sync Music, to discover and connect to your other nearby devices running MUSPLUS that you deliberately choose to pair with a PIN.
  • Files access — to import audio files you select from the Files app. Files are copied into the App; we never see or access anything else in your Files app.

What we collect

Nothing. The App contains no analytics, no advertising, no tracking, no crash-reporting service, and no third-party SDKs. We do not receive, store, or see any of your data — music files, metadata, artwork, or usage — because none of it is ever transmitted to us.

In-app purchases

The Tip Jar offers optional, one-time tips. Purchases are processed entirely by Apple under Apple's own privacy policy; we never learn who tipped, and the App contains no purchase history that reaches us.

Children

MUSPLUS contains no content or features directed at children and collects no data from anyone, including children.

Changes

If a future version of the App ever changes how data is handled, we will update this policy and the App Privacy section of the App Store listing before that version ships.

Your rights

Because MUSPLUS stores everything on your device and transmits nothing to us, we hold no personal data about you — there is no account, no server, and no record of you on our side. In practice that means your GDPR rights of access, rectification, erasure, restriction, portability and objection are things you exercise directly on your own device:

  • Access and portability — your music and playlists are files on your own device at all times.
  • Rectification — edit any track's metadata in the App at any time.
  • Erasure — delete individual tracks, use Settings → Clear all music, or delete the App, which removes all of its local data.

If you believe we hold personal data about you regardless, contact us at the address above and we will respond within 30 days. You also have the right to lodge a complaint with your national supervisory authority — in Slovenia, the Information Commissioner (Informacijski pooblaščenec, ip-rs.si).
